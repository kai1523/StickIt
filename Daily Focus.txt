## Daily focus actions

### Quick resets
Start a 25-minute focus session
Close one browser tab you no longer need
Take a five-minute screen break

#### Planning notes
Write down the next physical action
Review today's top three priorities
Turn a vague task into a short checklist

##### Highest priority
Send a concise progress update
Save a useful link with a short note
Prepare tomorrow's first task
Celebrate one thing you finished
