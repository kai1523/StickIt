## Welcome to StickIt
## This guide is also a live demo: ordinary lines become buttons you can click to copy.
## Instructions and utility descriptions are comments, so they are not clickable.

## Text file formatting
## Every nonblank line that does not start with at least two # characters is a copy button.
## A line without a TAB is displayed and copied exactly as written.
Copy this entire line when clicked
## Blank lines are ignored and create no button.
## Spaces on button lines are preserved in the copied text.
## A single leading # is not a comment marker; it remains part of a clickable line.
#This is a button because there is only one leading hash

## TAB-separated button format
## Put one TAB between the copied value and the button label.
## Text before the first TAB is copied; text after it is displayed as the button label.
demo-copy-value-123	Click to copy the demo value
## Only the first TAB separates the copy value from the label; later TABs stay in the label.
first-part-is-copied	This is the label	and this part stays in the label
## Hover a labeled button to preview its copied value.

## Comment heading levels
## Prefix a comment with 2–5 # characters (leading spaces are okay). The # markers are hidden in the app.
## Comment lines are unclickable, unindented, and have no background.
## A comment starts a section that continues until the next comment.
## Level 1 — two hashes, muted neutral blue-gray, smallest comment text.
## This is a level 1 comment. It has no strong accent.
### Level 2 — three hashes, teal accent.
### This is a level 2 comment with a teal accent.
#### Level 3 — four hashes, lavender accent and larger text.
#### This is a level 3 comment with a lavender accent.
##### Level 4 — five hashes, coral-pink accent, largest and strongest style.
##### This is a level 4 comment with the strongest accent.
## More than five leading hashes use the highest comment style.

## Search utility
## Click the magnifying glass in the top bar to open or hide the search field.
## Search spans every top-level TXT file beside StickIt; matches include comments, button labels, and copied values.
## Results are grouped by filename. A matching comment shows its section; a matching button shows its comment heading for context.
## Matching text is highlighted. A button matched only by its copied value gets a full-label accent.
## The magnifier stays blue while a query is active, even when the field is hidden.
## Click the magnifier again to hide or reopen the field without clearing the query.
## Click the × in the search field to clear the query and restore the selected file.

## File list and refresh utilities
## The left panel lists top-level TXT files from the app folder. Click a filename to show that file's buttons.
## StickIt watches the folder and updates after files are added, edited, renamed, or removed.
## Click the circular-arrow button between Search and Settings to refresh manually.
## Drag a TXT file onto the left panel to copy it into the app folder. Duplicate names receive _2, _3, and higher suffixes.
## Right-click a filename for Open in editor or Show in Explorer.
## The selected filename is highlighted in the file list.

## Copy and window utilities
## Click any button to copy its value. The bottom status bar confirms what was copied.
## The window stays on top. Drag an empty part of the top bar to move it.
## The − button minimizes StickIt; the × button at the top right closes it.
## Resize the window from any edge or corner. Window size, position, and left-panel width are remembered.
## Click the StickIt button at the top of the file panel to collapse into a 68 × 68 sticky-note bubble.
## Drag the upper half of the bubble to move it. Click the lower half to restore the main window.
## The version number appears at the bottom right.

## Settings utilities
## Click the gear icon to open Settings. Click the gear or outside the dropdown to close it.
## Wrap makes long button labels grow vertically so their full text can be read.
## Fade makes the unfocused window translucent and hides its text; focusing the window restores its contents.
## Fade is off by default. The − and + buttons lower or raise unfocused opacity in 10% steps.
## Settings remember the selected file, Wrap and Fade, opacity, window size and position, and left-panel width.
