Made to stay out of the way

Small window. Useful details.

01

One-click copy

Click a button to copy its value. Add a tab to show a short label while keeping the full value in the clipboard.

02

Search across lists

Find text in comments, labels, and copied values. Results keep their file and section context.

03

Edit where you work

Update lists in StickIt or in your text editor. Changes are picked up automatically.

04

Stay compact

Keep StickIt on top, resize it to fit, or fold it into a small sticky-note bubble.

Your content, your files

Easy to read.
Easy to change.

StickIt reads top-level .txt files beside the app. Keep them in version control, edit them with any text editor, or use StickIt’s built-in editor.

Lines become buttons. Comment headings create readable sections. Lists stay portable because their content is plain text.
